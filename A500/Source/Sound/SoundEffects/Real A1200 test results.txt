SoundEffect.s
  On first press (of fire button) after reset (or power up), it plays sample twice.
  Afterwards it is fine (i.e. on subsequent button presses when program still running and every single time when program re-run).
  Uses level 4 interrupt to stop the sound.  Works well but won't stop a playing sound part way through.

SoundEffect2.s
  Fine every time.
  Uses a dbra delay (does not use level 4 interrupt).  Not ideal for a game as CPU time is wasted.  Works well and can stop a playing sound part way through.

SoundEffect3.s
  Fine every time.
  Based upon Interphase code.
  Uses level 4 interrupt to start/stop the sound and also uses the first available sound channel.  Works well but won't stop a playing sound part way through.


SoundEffectTest.s

SoundEffectTest2.s

  Afterwards it plays sample once (i.e. when program re-run).
