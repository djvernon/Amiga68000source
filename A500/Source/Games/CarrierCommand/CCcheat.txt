Amiga Cheat Mode
----------------
Pause the game then type "THE BEST IS YET TO BE" then press '+' on numeric keypad.
(+/- then toggles Manta and Walrus invincibility)

During the game, pause and press the following keys on the numeric keypad:-
1 - refuel Manta or Walrus (only on information screen).
2 - repair damage to Manta or Walrus.
3 - send Carrier/Manta/Walrus immediately to destination.
6 - show colour palette.
7 - time goes quicker.
9 - show current difficulty level.

On the title screen:-
'+' and '-' on keypad cycle through objects.
Escape toggles power light on or off.
6 on keypad displays the colour palette.
7 pauses the animation.
